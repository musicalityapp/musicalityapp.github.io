The distance between two notes in an interval is measured in half steps and/or whole steps.

Intervals vary in shape across the keyboard, but the number of the steps remain consistent. For this reason, we don’t rely on the visual shape of an interval on the keyboard (unlike on guitar and bass). Instead, we rely on the distance between the notes, measured in steps.

The distance is always the same (four half steps), but the visual pattern of white and black keys changes.


In this stage, you'll focus on not just hearing intervals but also locating them. Now that you can identify intervals by ear, the next step is to learn the number of steps needed to create a specific interval on the keyboard.
